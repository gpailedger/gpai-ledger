## How Meta publishes Art. 53(1)(d) summaries

The transparency hub (transparency.meta.com/reports/regulatory-transparency-reports/) sits
behind a cookie-consent wall and renders its report catalog from embedded JSON. The
"EU AI Act Transparency Reports" series entries carry **signed, expiring CDN URLs**
(`scontent-*.fbcdn.net`, `oh=`/`oe=` params) — no stable document URLs exist on the hub or in its embedded JSON.
Consequences: ordinary crawlers and the Wayback Machine can't durably reference these
documents, and every capture requires re-extracting the current signed URL from the hub.
The extractor keys versions on the stable CDN path so rotating signatures don't mint
versions but content changes do.

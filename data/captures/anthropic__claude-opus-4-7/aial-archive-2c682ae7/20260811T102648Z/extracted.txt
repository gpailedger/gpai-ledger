Purposes  of  the  crawler(s):
ClaudeBot  collects  web  content  that  could  potentially  contribute  to  the  model’s  training.
General  description  of  crawler  behaviour:
We  aim  to  minimize  disruption  to  website  owners  and  be  thoughtful  about  how  quickly  ClaudeBot  crawls  domains,    including  by  respecting  crawl-delay  and  disallow  directives  in  robots.txt  ﬁles,  where  appropriate,  and  respecting  anti-circumvention  technologies  such  as  paywalls,  password  protection,  and  CAPTCHAs.  More  information  on  our  crawlers  and  how  they  work  can  be  found  at:  https://support.claude.com/en/articles/8896518-does-anthropic-crawl-data-from-the-web-and-how-can-site-owners-block-the-crawler.

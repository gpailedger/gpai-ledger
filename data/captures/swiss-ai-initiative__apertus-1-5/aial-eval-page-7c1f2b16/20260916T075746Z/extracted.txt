If you update your public summary, please
let us know

FAQ section

The public summary for the Apertus 1.5 model family can be found as a PDF on HuggingFace in the same context as its models, for instance at
the repository of Apertus-v1.5-70B
. This repository is gated and requires providing contact information, however
the summary can also be found in a publicly accessible manner on an Apertus's GitHub repository
. Despite declaring use of crawlers, the summary does not mention any further details regarding how the crawlers were used. Aside from this, the training data summary for Apertus 1.5 largely meets the same standards as the summary for Apertus v1.0. We assessed its score to be

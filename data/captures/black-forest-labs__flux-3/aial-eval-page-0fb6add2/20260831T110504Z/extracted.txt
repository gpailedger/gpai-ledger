The training data summary for FLUX.3 adheres to the structure of the template and provides various pieces of useful information for rightsholders. However, its descriptions still fail to meet the standards of the template at times, resulting in the primary loss of points. Notable fields in which further detail could be provided are the description of the linguistic characteristics of the overall training data, the description of other relevant characteristics of the overall training data, and the General description of non-publicly known private datasets obtained from third parties.

Evaluation Notes

If you update your public summary, please let us know so that we can update the evaluation and scores. We also welcome suggestions on improvements to this page, e.g. see FAQ section.

The public summary for Gemma 4 can be found on a GoogleAPIs storage, linked from Google's Transparency Report hub. The summary matches the structure of the template, and differs only minimally from the training data summary of the Gemini 3 Pro family of models. Points are lost primarily for providing high-level information where more detailed information is required, for instance in the field where a list of large publicly available data sources should be provided where the provider answers instead that "Our publicly available datasets include data across various sectors such as educational, government, legal, and research sectors comprising a wide variety of media types and languages". We assessed the summary's score to be 59.54% with Grade C for transparency, and 53.6% with Grade C for usefulness.

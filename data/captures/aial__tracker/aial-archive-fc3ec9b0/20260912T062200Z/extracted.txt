Modeldependencies
The Luciole base models are pre-trained from scratch by the provider. Their training
did not involve modification or fine-tuning of third-party model weights. There are
accordingly no general-purpose AI models already placed on the Union market on
which the base models depend. All models were trained using version 2.3.1 of the
NeMolibrary.

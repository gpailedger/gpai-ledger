Evaluation Notes

If you update your public summary, please let us know so that we can update the evaluation and scores. We also welcome suggestions on improvements to this page, e.g. see FAQ section.

The training data summary for Command A Plus generally describes the purposes for which data is included over the content of training data. Descriptions are generally high-level, and it is difficult for rightsholders to interpret provided information in an actionable manner. The summary scores 55.66% with Grade C for transparency and 51.28% with Grade C for usefulness.

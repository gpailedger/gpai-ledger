Evaluation Notes

If you update your public summary, please

The public summary for the FastwebMIIA models can be found
 on Fastweb's site
. The summary very approximately matches the structure of the template, and is provided in Italian. Several fields are missing or consolidated, causing significant loss of transparency. The summary indicates dataset as crawlers, causing further loss of scores. For the rest, the template provides a summary for which good-faith is evident and which describes data processing in detail. We assessed its score to be
50.06%
 with Grade
C
 for
transparency
, and
73.23%
 with Grade
C+
 for
usefulness
